GREEDY
Idea:
At every step → make the best local choice → commit → move forward.
BEST NOW → TAKE IT → DON'T LOOK BACK


Golden rule ⭐
Greedy = Local best choice + proof that it leads to global best answer.

How to recognize
Think Greedy when problem asks:
- max / min
- earliest / latest
- fewest / most
- scheduling
- selecting
- assigning
- intervals

Main question
What is the best choice RIGHT NOW?

Then ask:
Why is this choice safe?

If you cannot justify it → Greedy may be wrong.

choose → commit → move on
